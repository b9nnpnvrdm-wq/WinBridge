Get started

  1. Unzip the download

    Move WinBridge.app to Applications and double-click it.

  2. Use built-in Wine

    The included Wine engine requires Rosetta on Apple Silicon.

  3. Choose an app or game

    In Windows Apps, choose an EXE or MSI. In Steam, follow the setup for Windows games.
